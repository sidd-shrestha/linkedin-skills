# Content Strategy Templates

Four strategy blueprints mapped to the 4-tier depth model. Each includes the
pattern, when to use it, the asset pipeline, and an example posting sequence.

---

## S1: Expand (Seed → Pillar)

**When:** The idea is one sentence with no supporting structure. It needs to be
built out before it can be posted.

**Pipeline:** Raw seed → post-writer (expansion prompt) → single post in best
format → humanizer audit → publish

**Expansion structure (post-writer prompt):**

```
Topic: [seed]
Goal: reach / authority / leads / comments
Tone: [user's voice]
Format: text / carousel / poll

Structure:
1. Hook line — make the seed specific and relatable
2. Context — 2-3 sentences framing the problem
3. Stakes — why this matters to the reader
4. Evidence — one data point or example
5. Takeaway — the one thing to remember
6. CTA — question or soft ask
```

**Example:** Seed "AI tools are getting too expensive" → expanded post with
pricing comparison table, tier breakdown by team size, and a poll asking what
readers pay.

## S2: Single-Post (Sprout → Publish)

**When:** The idea is a complete thought but only fills one post. No expansion
needed, no material for derivatives.

**Pipeline:** Sprout → format-optimizer (format recommendation) → post-writer
(hook + body) → humanizer audit → publish

**Format decision (from format-optimizer):**

| Content type | Best format | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Personal story | Text-only | Story flows best without breaks |
| Opinion / hot take | Text-only | Debate needs full argument |
| Quick tip / insight | Text + image | Scanability + visual anchor |
| Data point | Text + image or carousel | Numbers benefit from visual |
| Community question | Poll | Low-friction participation |
| How-to (1-2 steps) | Text | Short enough for one post |

**Example:** Sprout "Cold outreach open rates went from 38% to 62% by
personalizing the first line" → single text post with hook, method, result, and
a "try this" CTA.

## S3: Atomize (Tree → Pillar + Derivatives)

**When:** The idea has multiple angles, data points, and enough material for a
pillar piece plus 3-5 derivative posts across 1-2 weeks.

**Pipeline:** Tree → post-writer (pillar article) → extraction of 3-5 derivatives
→ sequence scheduling → per-post humanizer audit → publish across 1-2 weeks

### Asset pipeline

| # | Asset | Format | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pillar article | Long-form text or article | Full idea, complete framework |
| 2 | Framework carousel | 6-8 slide PDF | Extract the core framework/steps |
| 3 | Counterintuitive finding | Text post | The most surprising insight |
| 4 | Poll | Poll | Core tension / question for audience |
| 5 | One-question close | Text post | "Ask your employer/boss/team this" |
| 6 (opt) | Behind-the-scenes | Short text or image | Personal angle on the research |

### Example sequence (2 weeks)

| Day | Post | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 Mon | Pillar: The complete EPF transfer guide | Long-form text |
| Week 1 Wed | Framework: Two pathways for EPF transfer | Carousel (6 slides) |
| Week 1 Fri | Poll: Did your employer contribute from day one? | Poll |
| Week 2 Tue | Finding: Probation doesn't exempt EPF contributions | Text post |
| Week 2 Thu | Question: One thing to ask your new employer | Text post |

### Extraction guide

To identify derivative material in a Tree:

1. **Counterintuitive finding** — What surprised you? Lead with that.
2. **Framework/steps** — Is there a process? Extract it as carousel slides.
3. **The question** — What one question does this raise for the reader?
4. **The data point** — What number would make someone stop scrolling?
5. **The story** — Is there a personal anecdote embedded in the material?
6. **The objection** — What would someone disagree with? Write that hot take.

## S4: Serialize (Forest → Multi-Week Series)

**When:** The material spans multiple distinct topics, each rich enough for its
own Tree-level treatment.

**Pipeline:** Forest → content-planner (series calendar) → per-week pillar
creation → per-week atomization → weekly publishing cadence

### Series structure

| Week | Topic (pillar) | Derivatives | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Highest-priority topic | Carousel + 2 text posts + poll | Establish authority |
| 2 | Second topic | Long-form + poll + text post | Build momentum |
| 3 | Third topic | Carousel + counterintuitive post | Vary format |
| 4 (opt) | Fourth topic | Text + poll + carousel | Close the series |

### Topic prioritization

| Priority factor | Weight | Signals |
|---|---|---|
| Audience interest | High | Previous engagement on similar topics |
| Timeliness | High | Current events, trending conversations |
| Personal authority | Medium | How much expertise the user has |
| Competitive gap | Low | How many others cover this topic |
| Lead potential | Medium | Does this topic attract the right audience |

### Example: 3-week Forest

**Source notes:** LinkedIn algorithm changes, cold outreach tactics, personal
brand building on a budget.

| Week | Pillar | Key derivative |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | How the 360Brew algorithm actually works in 2026 | Carousel: 5 signals that determine reach |
| 2 | Cold outreach that doesn't feel cold | Text post: one template, one result |
| 3 | Building a personal brand without a budget | Carousel: 6 free tactics |

## Strategy selection quick reference

| Tier | Strategy | Timeline | Posts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seed | Expand | 1 day | 1 |
| Sprout | Single-post | 1 day | 1 |
| Tree | Atomize | 1-2 weeks | 4-6 |
| Forest | Serialize | 2-4 weeks | 8-16 (2-4 pillars + derivatives) |
