This week I had to unlearn that.

The tool I was using (Claude Code) didn't want a precise script. It wanted a goal. You set it in motion and it starts a loop: explore, plan, code, commit. Like managing a junior engineer who works at 10x speed and never sleeps.

That shift — from "tell it how" to "tell it what" — was harder than any syntax I've dealt with.
